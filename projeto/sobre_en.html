OUR STORY

Since 2016,
care built on listening.

Farfalla was founded in 2016 by Renata Ferreira, with the idea of combining technical excellence and a human approach, in which every client is listened to with attention.

The name, which means butterfly in Italian, reflects the way we see care: a transformation that happens in each person's own time, without rushing and without imposed standards.

That is why every appointment begins with a conversation. We want to understand how you feel, what you expect and what your routine is like before suggesting any procedure. Only then, after a careful skin assessment, do we build a plan designed exclusively for you.

Beauty is feeling good in your own skin, in your own time
